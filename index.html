Pricing

What does painting cost?

Every home is different, which is why we quote in person and in writing. But you can get a realistic sense of the range before we ever visit. Here's what recent local projects cost, what moves the number, and how to read any quote you get, including ours.

Recent Richmond & Delta exteriors, as quoted

  • Steveston, Richmond · Full exterior repaint$9,000–$13,500
  • Tsawwassen, Delta · Full exterior repaint$11,000–$13,000
  • Seafair, Richmond · Luxury stucco exterior$16,000–$22,000

These are the same three projects pictured above. Interiors are quoted the same way: in person, itemized, in writing.

What moves the number

  • Size and height of the areas being painted
  • Condition of the surfaces and how much prep they need
  • Quality of paint you choose
  • Repairs like caulking, patching and minor wood work
  • How many colours are in the plan

What your written quote includes

  • Every surface and area, itemized line by line
  • Prep work spelled out, so nothing gets skipped
  • The exact paint products being used
  • The total, fixed in writing
  • Photos of what's included, so nothing is left to memory

Interiors and cabinets too

Walls, ceilings, trim and cabinet refinishing, quoted exactly the same way: in person, itemized, in writing.

How to tell a real quote from a vague one

If you're comparing quotes, the totals only mean something when the scopes match. Check every quote for these five things. Ours will have all five in writing.

If the quote just says
It should actually say
"Paint the exterior"
Every surface listed by name, with photos of what's included
"Prep included"
The actual prep, spelled out: washing, sanding, patching, caulking
"Premium paint"
The exact paint products being used
"We'll clean up after"
A clean site daily, and a final walkthrough together
"Price may change depending on conditions"
A fixed total that doesn't move after you approve it

Hold every painter to this standard, including us. If a quote can't show these five things, you can't know what the price actually buys.

Every job comes with a 2-year written workmanship warranty. If the paint fails because of our work, we come back and fix it.

Yes. Interior, exterior and cabinet painting across Richmond and Delta. The estimate and the written quote work exactly the same way.

A trained, uniformed Colour Craft crew with a crew leader running your job site. The people in your home are experienced, respectful and accountable. They arrive on time, introduce themselves, and take care with your property.

Not at all, most homeowners haven't. You don't need colours picked to get your quote, and we'll guide you through choosing them before the project starts.
