Pricing

What does painting cost?

Every home is different, which is why we quote in person and in writing. But you should know what drives the price, and exactly what your quote will include.

What drives the price

  • Size and height of the areas being painted
  • Condition of the surfaces and how much prep they need
  • Quality of paint you choose
  • Repairs like caulking, patching and minor wood work

What your written quote includes

  • Every surface and area, itemized line by line
  • Prep work spelled out, so nothing gets skipped
  • The exact paint products being used
  • A fixed total that doesn't change after you approve it

For reference, the three Richmond and Delta exteriors shown above ranged from $9,000 to $22,000. Your written quote gives you the exact number for your home.

FAQ

No. Your quote is a fixed written price. What you approve is what you pay. If you add work later, you approve a written change first.

Yes. WorkSafeBC coverage and $5 million in liability insurance. We can show you proof of both at your estimate.

What does the warranty cover?

Every job comes with a 2-year written workmanship warranty. If the paint fails because of our work, we come back and fix it.

How does the estimate work?

We come to your home, walk the project with you, and answer your questions. You get a detailed written quote with photos. There's no obligation and no pressure to decide on the spot.

Do you paint interiors too?

Yes. Interior, exterior and cabinet painting across Richmond and Delta. The estimate and the written quote work exactly the same way.

Who will actually be painting my home?

A trained, uniformed Colour Craft crew with a crew leader running your job site. The people in your home are experienced, respectful and accountable. They arrive on time, introduce themselves, and take care with your property.

We haven't chosen colours yet. Is that a problem?

Not at all, most homeowners haven't. You don't need colours picked to get your quote, and we'll guide you through choosing them before the project starts.

It changes through the season, and exterior projects need to finish before the fall rain. The estimate is free, so the safest move is booking it now and deciding after you have your quote.
