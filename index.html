Pricing

What does painting cost?

Every home is different, which is why we quote in person and in writing. But you should know what moves the number, and exactly what your written quote will include.

What moves the number

  • Size and height of the areas being painted
  • Condition of the surfaces and how much prep they need
  • Quality of paint you choose
  • Repairs like caulking, patching and minor wood work
  • How many colours are in the plan
  • Whether rooms are furnished and lived in, or empty

Your written quote is your price.

We visit your home, walk the project with you, and you have your detailed written quote, with photos, within 24 hours. The price you approve is the price you pay. No sign-today discounts, no pressure, no surprises at the end.

Comparing quotes

How to tell a real quote from a vague one

If you're comparing quotes, the totals only mean something when the scopes match. Check every quote for these five things. Ours will have all five in writing.

If the quote just says
It should actually say
"Paint the exterior"
Every surface listed by name, with photos of what's included
"Prep included"
The actual prep, spelled out: washing, sanding, patching, caulking
"Premium paint"
The exact paint products being used
"We'll clean up after"
A clean site daily, and a final walkthrough together
"Price may change depending on conditions"
A fixed total that doesn't move after you approve it

Hold every painter to this standard, including us. If a quote can't show these five things, you can't know what the price actually buys.
