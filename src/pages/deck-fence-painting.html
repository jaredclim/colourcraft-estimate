About This Service

Professional Deck & Fence Painting

Your deck and fence are constantly exposed to the sun, rain, and changing temperatures. Over time, this weather can cause wood to fade, crack, or even rot. Regular maintenance keeps your outdoor wood looking sharp and structurally sound. We help homeowners in Richmond and Delta protect their outdoor investments with painting and staining that shields exterior wood from moisture, UV exposure, and seasonal wear.

Maintenance note

Decks and fences typically require restaining every 2 to 3 years for semi-transparent stains, and every 3 to 5 years for solid stains or paint, depending on sun and moisture exposure. Regular maintenance keeps the wood protected and looking its best, and helps avoid more costly repairs down the road.

How deck and fence work is quoted

Exterior wood moves, weathers and holds moisture differently than siding, so deck and fence staining is quoted separately and is not covered by our 2-year workmanship warranty. Everything included is itemized in your written quote before any work starts, and we will tell you honestly if a surface needs repair or replacement rather than coating.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does deck staining or painting cost?

It depends on the size of the deck, the condition of the wood, how much cleaning and prep is required, and the product selected. We provide free, detailed on-site estimates with a fixed written price before any work starts.

Should I stain or paint my deck?

Stain is generally the better choice for decks because it penetrates the wood grain and lets moisture escape, so it wears rather than peels. Solid paint gives more colour coverage but can peel if moisture gets trapped underneath. We look at your deck's condition and tell you which one suits your wood and exposure.

How often should I stain or paint my deck?

Decks typically need restaining every 2 to 3 years for semi-transparent stains, and every 3 to 5 years for solid stains or paint. Keeping to that cycle slows graying and helps keep water out of the wood, which costs far less than repairs.

Is deck and fence work covered by your warranty?

No. Our 2-year written workmanship warranty covers house painting. Exterior wood weathers and moves in ways coatings cannot control, so deck and fence staining is quoted separately and excluded from that warranty. We would rather tell you that upfront than have you discover it later.

What is the best time of year to stain a deck?

Late spring through early fall, when the wood is dry and temperatures hold above the product's minimum. We will not apply stain to damp wood, because drying conditions decide whether the finish lasts.

What deck stain products do you use?

We use premium exterior wood finishes including Sherwin-Williams Woodscapes and Sikkens Cetol, chosen for penetration, UV protection and durability. We match the product to your wood species and the finish you want, and the exact products are listed in your quote.

Do you power wash and prep before staining?

Yes. Our process includes power washing, brightening with a wood cleaner, light sanding of rough areas, and letting the wood dry fully before any stain goes on. Skipping prep is the main reason deck stain fails early.

Can you stain a fence at the same time as the deck?

Yes, and it is usually the better way to do it. Combining them into one visit keeps the colour consistent across your outdoor spaces. We can include gates, pergolas and sheds in the same quote.
