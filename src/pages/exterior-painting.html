How long does exterior house painting last?

Exterior paint life depends on exposure, surface condition and prep. On the coast, a properly prepped repaint generally holds for years rather than seasons, which is why prep is where we spend the time. We use Sherwin-Williams exterior products engineered for moisture, UV exposure, and temperature swings, and we back our work with a 2-year warranty.

What is the best time of year to paint a house exterior?

The ideal window for exterior painting is late spring through early fall, May to October, when temperatures are consistently above 10°C and rain is minimal. We monitor forecasts closely and schedule around weather to ensure proper paint adhesion and a lasting finish.

Can you paint my exterior in rainy weather?

We do not paint in the rain or when surfaces are wet, as moisture prevents proper adhesion and curing. We watch the forecast and move your dates if the weather turns, and we tell you the new ones. We use that time to prep, prime, and protect surfaces so we're ready to paint the moment conditions are right.

Do you repair damaged wood or siding before painting?

Yes. Every exterior job includes an inspection and minor repairs: patching cracks, caulking gaps, replacing small sections of damaged wood, and spot-priming bare areas. Anything larger is written into your quote before we start, so there are no surprises later.

What exterior paint products do you use?

We use Sherwin-Williams premium exterior systems including Duration, SuperPaint and Emerald, products chosen for their durability, coverage, and performance in varied climates. We match the right product to each surface type and exposure level, and the exact products for your home are listed in your written quote.

Is exterior painting worth the investment?

A professional exterior repaint protects your home from moisture damage, UV degradation, and wood rot, which can save far more than the paint costs in structural repairs. It also improves curb appeal and resale value.
