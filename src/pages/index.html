What a repaint here actually costs

Full exterior repaints in Richmond and Delta generally land somewhere between $9,000 and $22,000, depending on the size of the home, how much prep the surfaces need, and the products you choose. That spread is wide because homes are, which is why we measure in person and put one fixed number in writing rather than quoting from a table. Interiors are quoted exactly the same way.

What moves the number

  • Size and height of the areas being painted
  • Condition of the surfaces and how much prep they need
  • Quality of paint you choose
  • Repairs like caulking, patching and minor wood work
  • How many colours are in the plan
  • Whether rooms are furnished and lived in, or empty
