Pricing

What does painting cost?

Every home is different, which is why we quote in person and in writing. But you should know the range, and what moves the number within it.

Your written quote is your price.

We measure your home and run it through the same estimating system on every job, so your price comes from the surfaces, the hours and the materials, not from a gut feel on the day. The price you approve is the price you pay. If we find something like rot behind the trim, we show you first and price it in writing before we touch it.

These are averages, not quotes. Larger or more complex projects can run higher. We measure in person and put one fixed number in writing.

What moves the number

  • Size of the areas being painted
  • Amount and complexity of the trim
  • Condition of the surfaces and prep required
  • Type of surface, like stucco, wood or vinyl
  • How easy the home is to access and work around
  • Quality of paint and number of coats needed
