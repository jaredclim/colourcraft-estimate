Frequently Asked Questions

How much does interior painting cost?

Interior painting costs vary with the number of rooms, ceiling height, surface condition, and the finish you choose. Rather than quote a per-room figure that may not fit your home, we measure in person and put a fixed price in writing. The estimate is free and there is no obligation.

Every house painting project carries a 2-year written workmanship warranty, WorkSafeBC coverage and $5 million liability insurance.

How long will my interior project take?

It depends on the number of rooms, the amount of prep and how many coats each surface needs. We give you a defined schedule with your written quote before the project starts, and you'll know each day what is happening next.

Do I need to move my furniture?

We handle furniture covering and floor protection with drop cloths and plastic sheeting. If you have delicate or irreplaceable items you would rather move yourself, tell us at the estimate and we'll plan around them. We leave your space clean and organized at the end of every day.

What interior paint products do you use?

We use Sherwin-Williams premium interior lines including Emerald, Cashmere and Duration Home, chosen for washability and coverage. We recommend the right sheen for each surface and room, and the exact products for your home are listed in your written quote.

Do you repair walls before painting?

Yes. Interior prep includes filling nail holes, patching cracks, sanding rough areas, and priming bare spots. Proper prep is what separates a professional finish from a DIY one, and it is written into your quote rather than left to assumption.

Can I stay in my home while you paint?

Yes. We can work room by room, protect your belongings, use low-VOC paints to keep odour down, and clean up at the end of each day so the disruption stays manageable.

We haven't chosen colours yet. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We'll guide you during the estimate. Your local paint store also has free colour tools, and many clients pick their palette between the estimate and the start date.
